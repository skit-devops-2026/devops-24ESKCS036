About BreadBasket

Connecting surplus food with communities in need.

Our Mission

BreadBasket was built to bridge the gap between food waste and hunger. We strive to create a seamless platform where local businesses, donors, and NGOs can connect quickly to redistribute excess food efficiently.

Who We Are

We are a dedicated two-person team passionate about using web technology to solve real-world problems. BreadBasket is our initiative designed to make food sharing simple, transparent, and accessible for everyone.

How It Works

By linking local food providers directly with non-profit organizations, our system helps ensure surplus meals are picked up and delivered fresh to those who need them most.
